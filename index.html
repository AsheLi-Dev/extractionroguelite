    Game Instructions

    Objective

    Navigate through 5 maps, defeat enemies, collect loot, and defeat the final boss to save your character!

    Controls

    • WASD or Arrow Keys - Move your character
    • Mouse Click - Attack in the direction of your mouse
    • Space - Dash (has cooldown)
    • E - Interact with objects (shrines, chests, doors)
    • I - Open inventory
    • Esc or P - Pause game

    Gameplay

    • Combat: Click to attack enemies. Different skills have unique effects and cooldowns.
    • Leveling: Defeat enemies to gain XP. Level up to choose upgrades that enhance your abilities.
    • Loot: Collect equipment items that drop from enemies. Equip them to boost your stats.
    • Dash: Use Space to dash and avoid damage. You're briefly invincible during dash.
    • Health: Watch your health bar. If it reaches zero, the run ends.

    Maps & Exploration

    • Progress through 5 different maps, each with unique themes and enemies.
    • Find exits to move to the next map.
    • Explore sub-areas like ruins, rooms, and mazes for guaranteed rewards.
    • Discover shrines that offer powerful upgrades or remove penalties.
    • Watch out for environmental obstacles that block movement and projectiles.

    Sub-Areas

    • Ruins: Open structures with enemies. Defeat all enemies to unlock the chest.
    • Rooms: Enclosed areas with locked doors. Clear all enemies to unlock the exit and claim rewards.
    • Mazes: Winding corridors with enemies and loot. Navigate to the center or exit for rewards.

    Tips

    • Use dash strategically to avoid enemy attacks and reposition.
    • Different skills work better in different situations - experiment!
    • Collect and equip items to increase your power.
    • Level up upgrades can dramatically change your playstyle.
    • Environmental obstacles can provide cover from enemy projectiles.
    • Sub-areas offer guaranteed rewards - explore them when you can!
