🎨 Sprite Atlas Measurement Tool

How to use:

  1. Click on a sprite from the list on the right to select it
  2. On the canvas, click and drag to draw a rectangle around the sprite
  3. The coordinates will be automatically saved for that sprite
  4. Repeat for all sprites
  5. Click "Copy Output Code" to copy the SPRITE_COORDS object
  6. Paste it into src/entities/enemy.js to replace the empty SPRITE_COORDS object
